# Conformal and calibration correction (2026-11-07)

## Change

The split-conformal implementation now selects the finite-sample upper order statistic directly. The prior quantile conversion could select a rank below the prescribed rank. Calibration labels and probability arrays are validated before use. Prediction evaluation and reliability summaries now report both the legacy unweighted macro-bin ECE (`ece_uniform`) and sample-weighted equal-width-bin ECE (`ece_weighted_uniform`). Existing keys and historical artifacts remain available.

## New source-side runs

The corrected implementation was rerun on the existing Heart5/Five States and 2021 Mendeley data using seeds 42–51. These are internal repeated-split results; they do not constitute independent external validation. Target site labels were not used for these reruns.

| Dataset | Candidate | Conformal coverage | Macro-bin ECE | Weighted ECE | AURC | Confidence-error AUC |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Five States | Corrected OSEC | 0.908 | 0.121 | 0.062 | 0.0505 | 0.761 |
| Five States | Stable OSEC | 0.909 | 0.121 | 0.060 | 0.0516 | 0.760 |
| Mendeley | Corrected OSEC | 0.906 | 0.221 | 0.031 | 0.0041 | 0.905 |
| Mendeley | Stable OSEC | 0.908 | 0.217 | 0.029 | 0.0042 | 0.905 |

Compared with the prior 10-seed artifacts, corrected-quantile coverage shifted from 0.913 to 0.908 on Five States and from 0.914 to 0.906 on Mendeley. This confirms that the previous point estimates must not be presented as results from the corrected implementation. Weighted ECE is much lower than macro-bin ECE on Mendeley because the macro measure gives each occupied bin equal weight regardless of the number of observations. The candidate linkage differences remain small and do not show a consistent stable-selector reliability advantage.

## Limits

The runs use previously examined development cohorts and repeated random splits. They do not establish patient-independent generalization, real-site distribution-shift detection, clinical safety, decision utility, or conformal coverage under dataset shift. The Mendeley target reference standard and predictor timing remain unresolved. No external approvals, author declarations, data rights, or validation claims are inferred from this correction.
