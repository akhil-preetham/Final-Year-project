## Supported framing

Present CARDIODIAG as a methodological evaluation of an existing research prototype, reproducibility and limitations under cohort shift, with a negative result for dependable unseen-site OOD/selective behavior. No clinical utility, deployment, safety, universal superiority or independent generalization claim is supported. The paper must clearly separate internal evaluation, retrospective stress tests and synthetic diagnostics.

## Recommendation and preparation

**Primary recommendation: BMJ Health & Care Informatics**, conditional on author approval, ethics determination, and a manuscript emphasizing a careful healthcare-informatics reliability evaluation. It offers a more realistic scope match than a methods journal demanding generalizable algorithmic novelty. Keep the manuscript's current nonclinical claims and report external generalization as not demonstrated. Budget and waiver eligibility remain unresolved; authors must decide before submission.

Prepare title page, structured abstract and standard original-research sections against the current official BMJ author instructions. Retain reproducibility detail and limitations; include data/code availability statements that explain access constraints. Do not submit until the author forms, ethics determination, funding/conflict statements, data rights and venue are approved. Manuscript itself is `paper/manuscript.tex` and remains a generic research draft pending approval.
