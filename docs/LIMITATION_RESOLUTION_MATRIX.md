# CARDIODIAG limitation-resolution matrix

**Updated:** 2026-11-05. This matrix records the current offline engineering
scope. Clinical deployment is not a completion gate. A limitation is marked
solved only where the saved evidence supports the corresponding claim.

| Limitation | Status | Current evidence | Why it remains / what completes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-split preprocessing, resampling, or feature selection in historical notebooks | Solved with evidence for the corrected path | Historical leak-prone paths are preserved and labelled; corrected transformations and selectors are inside training pipelines and nested folds; automated leakage checks pass. | Do not reuse historical scores as corrected results. Exact reproduction of the old path remains a separate limitation. |
| Direct target, identifier, or fold leakage in the corrected evaluation | Solved with evidence for the audited runs | Target and ID columns are separated/dropped; target-derived AngioCAD fields are excluded; selection and hyperparameter tuning are fold-local; all 106 collected tests pass. | Re-audit if the data adapter, feature schema, or experiment protocol changes. |
| Faithful historical LoRAS/XAIH-DRC reproduction | Cannot be verified | Archived notebooks disagree on split, preprocessing, model, search, LIME/PCA, and resampling order; the LoRAS package/parameters and source table lineage are not frozen. | Obtain the original executable environment, exact parameters, artifact-to-table mapping, and author confirmation. Otherwise retain the forensic approximation and never call it exact reproduction. |
| Stability-aware feature-selection benefit | Partially addressed | Matched nested comparisons and stability/reliability linkage show no consistent benefit; excluding Mendeley's fluoroscopy-derived feature barely changes its high internal AUC. | Keep the negative/mixed finding. Independent replication is required before generalizing it beyond these benchmarks. |
| Calibration under dataset shift | Partially addressed | Source-only calibration is implemented. AngioCAD calibrated ECE is 0.216; paired calibration improvements are inconclusive. | Freeze a calibration policy and assess it on new untouched compatible sites. Do not re-tune against already examined AngioCAD labels. |
| Feature-only OOD recognition | Partially addressed | AngioCAD OOD AUROC is 0.589 (95% conditional interval 0.541–0.635); age/sex-only sensitivity is 0.544 (0.496–0.592). The trusted-site gate withholds scores for unknown sites. | The metadata gate is a conservative operational guard, not a learned detector. Improve or claim feature-only detection only after source-only method development and untouched multi-site testing. |
| Selective prediction and conformal coverage under shift | Partially addressed | Separate source calibration/conformal partitions, selective metrics, and fail-closed inference exist; post-hoc AngioCAD replay automatically covered 5.6%. | Low coverage and target reuse prevent a positive reliability claim. Set useful operating targets before evaluating new sites. |
| Endpoint-aligned external generalization | Requires new data | One source-distinct aligned site (AngioCAD; 377 matched records) was evaluated: AUC 0.787 (95% interval 0.734–0.838), with poor calibration and weak feature-only OOD separation. | Obtain additional endpoint-compatible sites, freeze the model and feature map before labels are opened, and report each site separately. A single site cannot estimate site-to-site variance. |
| Patient-level overlap between source and external cohorts | Cannot be verified | Public files lack common patient identifiers; site provenance differs, but that cannot prove person-level independence. | Ask data controllers for privacy-preserving linkage or a documented source-level overlap attestation. Do not claim patient-level independence without it. |
| Mendeley target definition and predictor measurement time | Cannot be verified | The official codebook defines presence/absence only. Same-split exclusion of `noofmajorvessels` leaves all five AUCs between 0.993 and 0.996; it does not identify the diagnostic reference standard or timing of other fields. | Obtain the original data dictionary or author confirmation. Until then, use Mendeley only as retrospective replication evidence, not endpoint-confirmatory or early-detection evidence. |
| AngioCAD FBS unit and missing clinical rows | Cannot be verified | The Zenodo feature workbook contains an `FBS` column but no units; the public release has 413 labels and 377 matched feature rows. An age/sex-only sensitivity gives similar AUC (0.7868 vs 0.7877) but cannot establish units; 36 feature rows are absent. | Obtain the source data dictionary/author confirmation and request the missing rows. If unavailable, use the age/sex-only sensitivity as post-hoc context and state the restriction. |
| Statistical uncertainty and subgroup inference | Partially addressed | Five-seed internal sensitivity and conditional cohort bootstrap intervals are reported. They are not patient-level or between-site intervals. | Require patient IDs/group structure and more independent sites for patient-level and site-level intervals. Avoid fairness or subgroup claims where sample sizes/attributes do not support them. |
| Robustness and explanations | Partially addressed | Synthetic perturbation and explanation/stability artifacts exist; they are exploratory and largely internal. | Pre-register realistic perturbations and validate on independent cohorts before making robustness or explanation-reliability claims. |
| Full A–L matched factorial attribution | Partially addressed | Corrected baselines, OVEC/OSEC, stable selectors, AURA-ML, calibration, conformal/selective policies, and transfer are represented in separate artifacts; the primary five-candidate comparison shares nested folds. | A complete factorial comparison cannot be reconstructed for unavailable original LoRAS/LIME branches and is not one common experiment. Keep component claims narrow; extend the common-fold design only for implementable components and a stated compute budget. |
| Full five-candidate nested run on the 70K Kaggle benchmark | Partially addressed | Separate 70K candidate, tuning, stability/linkage, and reduced corrected-vs-stable runs exist; the full custom nested run was not completed. | This target/schema is incompatible with the primary angiographic endpoint and does not add external-validation evidence. Run only if a separate benchmark question justifies the computational cost and rights are cleared; it is not a blocker for the declared shift-evaluation prototype. |
| Data licenses and redistribution | Partially addressed | UCI Heart Disease is officially CC BY 4.0; Mendeley and AngioCAD releases identify CC BY 4.0. The Kaggle 70K page reports `Unknown`. Heart5 rows map to UCI records, but the composite's own transformation/license notice is absent. | Retain attribution, do not redistribute 70K data/results or the composite until upstream terms and transformation provenance are reviewed. A clean code-only release can exclude raw data and restricted derived artifacts. |
| Reproducibility of the corrected prototype | Solved with evidence | Locked environment, hashes, saved predictions/splits, 106 passing tests, lint, compilation, and independent metric/sensitivity audits are recorded. | Preserve the manifest and rerun checks if code or data inputs change. This does not solve historical reproduction or external generalization. |
| Manuscript facts and venue requirements | Partially addressed | A 14-page draft is compiled, text-extracted on all pages, visually checked, and hash-recorded. Author names and a provisional shared affiliation are supplied. | Author order/individual affiliations, coauthor contributions, ethics determination, funding, conflicts/data-use statements, and target journal remain unconfirmed; verify before submission and then apply that venue's current instructions. |
| Clinical utility, prospective validation, and regulatory readiness | Not addressed | These were not performed and are outside the agreed final-year engineering-project completion scope. | Do not claim clinical use. These are future work only if the project later adopts a clinical intended use and obtains the appropriate partners, data, ethics, and regulatory review. |
