# CARDIODIAG open dataset and license audit

**Checked:** 2026-11-06. This is a documentary audit of the checkout and official repository records. “Open access” means a person can obtain a file under the repository’s access workflow; it does not itself establish permission to modify, publish, or redistribute it. This is research software/data provenance, not legal advice.

## Findings that affect the project

- CARDIODIAG currently has several datasets in active or documented experimental lineage. Some are research-only benchmarks, and some were already used as external-data stress tests. Their presence in the checkout does not make every source independently licensed.
- The project's 918-row `heart5.csv` is a derived composite with no standalone license or full transformation history. UCI’s CC BY 4.0 license applies to the UCI source material, but does not automatically settle the rights in the particular downstream Kaggle composite or remove third-party conditions. Do not redistribute that derived CSV until its precise lineage and terms are resolved.
- The 70,000-row Kaggle cardiovascular file has unknown license metadata in the project’s recorded source inspection. Treat as use-at-risk for redistribution; do not publish the raw file or derived row-level data absent source permission.
- The nine proposed candidate datasets are not interchangeable. UCI Heart Disease and Statlog overlap existing lineage; Z-Alizadeh Sani has already been evaluated. Heart failure, MI complications, and ECG waveform sets have different outcomes or modalities and would require distinct research tasks.
- None of these findings permits describing CARDIODIAG as clinically validated.

## Attribution and redistribution checklist

1. Preserve the exact source record URL, version, DOI, access date, source hash, and license text alongside every acquired dataset.
2. For CC BY 4.0, attribute the creators/source, name and link the license, state whether changes were made, preserve supplied notices, and do not imply endorsement. Check third-party materials separately.
3. For ODC-By, retain the attribution notice and comply with the license’s database-specific conditions; do not silently relabel the dataset CC BY.
4. For source terms that grant research use but do not clearly grant downstream redistribution (ACATH), ask the provider or publish only non-identifying aggregate results.
5. For restricted/DUA data (MultiD4CAD), obey the agreement, do not upload the data to this repository, and do not redistribute data or unauthorized derivatives.
6. Keep Kaggle license-unknown data and its row-level derivatives out of public releases until permission is established.
7. Dataset licenses do not waive privacy, ethics, institutional review, database, publicity, trademark, or third-party software rights.
