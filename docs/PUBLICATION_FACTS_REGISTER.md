## Reporting and appraisal tools

Use [TRIPOD+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378) as the reporting checklist; it has 27 main items and supersedes TRIPOD 2015 for prediction-model studies. Use [PROBAST+AI](https://www.bmj.com/content/388/bmj-2024-082505) to appraise development quality, evaluation risk of bias and applicability; it distinguishes model development from model evaluation. Neither checklist substitutes for suitable design or evidence.

## Submission gate

Journal submission is **BLOCKED** until all authors confirm order, affiliations, contributions, funding and competing interests; the institution determines ethics/consent language; data reuse rights and availability statements are verified; and a venue is chosen. Claims must remain limited to a reproducible research prototype with one external endpoint-aligned site and unresolved generalization.
