# Project-owner inputs and external resources required

| Needed item | Why it is required | Criticality blocked | Alternative | Exact action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Institutional ethics/IRB or exemption determination | Determines appropriate secondary-data research statement | Manuscript submission | Keep as “not determined” and do not claim exemption | Ask the PVPSIT research ethics authority/supervisor for a written determination covering secondary public datasets |
| Author order, individual affiliations, contributions and approval | Authorship must reflect actual contributions and agreement | Submission | Keep provisional metadata and do not submit | All listed authors confirm final order, affiliation, contribution statement and manuscript approval |
| Funding and competing-interest statements | Factual declarations require author knowledge | Submission | Mark pending | Each author confirms funding and conflicts in writing |
| Project-owned repository/release destination | Current local work is uncommitted in an original-source checkout | Reproducibility/public code release | Keep local snapshot; do not push | Owner selects/creates project-owned remote and confirms licensing scan before publication |
| Second untouched endpoint-compatible hospital cohort | Required for confirmatory unseen-site performance | Reliable transport claim | Maintain NO-GO; report AngioCAD as one-site evidence | A PI/institution obtains lawful cohort with codebook, units, endpoint, event counts and overlap process |
| Framingham clinical cohort access, if pursuing longitudinal risk redesign | Data request and endpoint redesign required | Alternative research question | Do not use as angiographic validation | A PI submits research application / BioLINCC request and obtains ethics determination |
| MIMIC-IV access, if pursuing EHR redesign | Credentialing, training and DUA are mandatory | Alternative research question | Do not use without approved access | Applicant completes PhysioNet credentialing/training and personally signs the relevant DUA |
| Dataset rights for derived 70K outputs | Existing scan does not settle redistribution rights | Data release | Exclude uncertain derived outputs | Confirm dataset license and rights for every derived file before packaging |

Independent local code/results work can continue without these inputs. Restricted data were not requested or acquired. See EXTERNAL_DATA_ACQUISITION_REPORT.md and PUBLICATION_FACTS_REGISTER.md.
